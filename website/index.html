Historical & Benchmark Presets:

Regional Hazard Identifies tectonic boundary & background seismicity (e.g. Ring of Fire vs stable plate).

Wave Attenuation Shallower foci (<30 km) direct peak shear energy into surface cities; deep events (>70 km) dampen ground motion.

Rupture Scale Subduction megathrusts hold the largest fault contact areas (producing M8.0 to M9.5+); normal faults cap lower.

Site Resonance Soft saturated clay traps and amplifies seismic waves up to 220%; hard crystalline bedrock dampens shaking.

Geometric Decay Ground motion decays exponentially with distance as seismic wavefronts spread spherically through crust.

Secondary Risk Offshore seafloor displacement generates tsunami waves, multiplying total disaster severity by 1.8x.

India Subcontinent Benchmark Presets:

Seismic Zone India spans Zone II (low risk, Peninsular Shield) to Zone V (very high risk, NE India & Andaman).

Regional Corridor Western margins (Gujarat/Rann of Kutch) to Eastern Himalayan Arc and Andaman Trench.

Focal Depth Shallow crustal events (<30 km) dominate the Himalayan Front; deep events (>100 km) occur in the Andaman subduction slab.

Network Coverage Number of seismic stations used to locate the event. Higher counts improve magnitude estimation reliability.

Station Distribution Gaps >180° indicate poor azimuthal coverage — the event is outside the network, reducing location accuracy.

Travel-Time Fit Root mean square of arrival time residuals. Lower values (<0.5s) indicate well-constrained hypocenters.
